Routing policy

Reset data comes from upstream responses. Accounts without a future reset use round robin when no known resets are available. Existing explicit configuration is preserved until you save.

Account priorities

Higher numbers are preferred within each provider. Saving a priority affects new threads and failover; active threads retain their available account. Codex subscription accounts use WebSocket by default.
